**<u>Acoustooptics. Slowness figure of LiNbO3</u>**

In crystals, the speed of an acoustic wave depends on direction. The angle through which light is diffracted is inversely proportional to the phase speed of the wave. Therefore, the diffraction pattern reproduces a cross-section of the slowness surface, which can be calculated using the Christoffel equation.
