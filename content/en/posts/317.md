Predatory fungi of Uzbekistan

I'm going to continue serious math. But not today. Let's take a short break.

Yesterday I fell in a poetic pit. Everything started with a short funny rhyme, published in a channel I read. I'm not sure how to translate it into English. It's a nice exercise. I'll do it. Next time.

The thing is very simple. You write a four-line piece of rhymed text and put "Predatory fungi of Uzbekistan" as the last line. It's just a type of joke I couldn't resist. After I found all such rhymes on the Internet and listened to the song based on these rhymes, my brain got tuned and I started to generate these rhymes myself. In the comments we can share our findings.

One of the rhymes was about codex and claude. It was about the fruitless efforts to get codex and claude to bring these "Predatory fungi of Uzbekistan". Suddenly it started to sound like a challenge. I ran these two guys in one git repository, gave them a starting point, asked them to work together and went AFK. You can check the [result](<https://tarstars.github.io/carnivore_mushrooms/en/game/>). If you want to crawl as a nematode in the soil of Uzbekistan, you are welcome to. Feel free to check the [whole site](<https://tarstars.github.io/carnivore_mushrooms/en/>). The game is probably the best one. But you'll probably like the board game or the video.
