ICFPC 2026. Tasks.

I always dreamed about after-match for ICFPC. When contest finishes I'm exhausted, but there are a lot of ideas and it's easy to think about this topic. Dreams came true. This time I cloned the contest site as [github pages mirror](<https://tarstars.github.io/>) .

As bonus I now have an opportunity to add my own problems on this site. You can check Fibonacci and Array Sum problems. Of course, it's a simplified version of the contest site, there is no backend part, you can't get scores for your solutions, but you can play with this weird 2D programming language.

I tried "Fibonacci" and "Array Sum" problems as slightly more complex version of introduction problems, but turned our that they are extremely simple. Nevertheless, the first problem "Triangle" turned out to be quite non-trivial without Gauss' trick.
