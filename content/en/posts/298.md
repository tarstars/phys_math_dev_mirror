**<u>Professional neuroslopper.</u>**

That's me.

Some time ago,  there was a popular meme: the same man sitting in the same pose in front of a computer, with different captions: "Work", "Study", "Entertainment". The last panel: he looks back on all these memories from his deathbed and dies happily.

That's basically my life now.
[
Learning](<https://github.com/tarstars/leetcode/tree/main/rust>) - 30 problems from leetcode in Rust with Claude. Working - codex + claude + fable. Having fun: [troll\_farm](<https://github.com/tarstars/troll_farm>) .

I already told you about the troll farm and icfpc2026. Now I'm somewhere in the middle. My progress stalled for about a month. Tons of commits, tokens burned, no visible progress. I focused on an agent coordination system loosely based on my ICFPC setup, with some elements borrowed from the Unagi team's approach. It's starting to work, and it seems that I'm finally making progress with my trolls, but it's too early for a full post.

So today, just a small piece of advice.

Codex is [still](<https://t.me/phys_math_dev/194>) an forgetful mad scientist. Opus is a diligent engineer. And there is a new player in the team: Fable.

In my setup:

- Fable - to coordinate the whole process, write blueprints, call subagents. It's really good in this.
- Opus - writes programs
- OpenAI Sol - reviews designs, plans, and completed work

By default, Fable has a rather annoying habit of being very chatty, using project-specific notation and overly abstract wording. If you struggle with it as I do, borrow this prompt:

"Give me a smart, short, and idiotically clear answer."
