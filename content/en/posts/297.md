**<u>What's on my plate right now</u>**: quite a demanding project at work, dental surgery, a [gentle intro to Rust](<https://github.com/tarstars/leetcode/tree/main/rust>), trolls farm analysis, icfpc2026 post-mortem, a court case over a driver's license. So,

**<u>Let's talk about a kite.</u>**

I used to build kites with my granddad. We made them the Soviet way: newspaper, string and thin wooden strips called *dranka*. This "dranka" is like one of those many things that disappeared with my childhood. Nowadays I can't find it at all, while it was almost everywhere 35 years ago.

The size of the kite is determined by pine strips I bought on wildberries - 300mm. In this spartan design everything serves some purpose. The tail - a strip of toilet paper - stabilizes the whole thing. String which bows the upper strip of the frame gives stability  in another plane. Three threads set the angle of attack.

A piece of cord, sheet of paper, some glue, pine strips, 40 minutes of the late summer evening - cheap. Holding the string as it dances as if you caught a fish - priceless.
